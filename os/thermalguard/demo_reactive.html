Under the Hood: Comparing the Two Governor Policies
Legacy Reactive Governor (Threshold Control)
Logic: Triggers only after a threshold is crossed: drops to 80% at 60°C and 30% at 70°C.
The Physics Flaw (Thermal Inertia): When writing at 2,500 MB/s, heating slope is +1.5 to 2.5°C/s. By the time it hits 70°C, accumulated heat causes thermal overshoot (72–74°C). Once cooled below 67°C, it releases back to full speed, creating an inefficient sawtooth oscillation.
ThermalGuard Predictive Governor (Rate-of-Rise dT/dt)
Logic: Computes the thermal derivative: dT/dt = ΔT/Δt.
The Solution (Lookahead Clamping): Projects temperature 5 seconds ahead: T_proj = T + (dT/dt)·5s. If projected temperature reaches 70°C, it pre-emptively clamps background bandwidth before reaching the limit. This achieves Zero Thermal Overshoot and a stable flat throughput line.
