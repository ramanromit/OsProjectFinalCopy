Under the Hood: How the Heuristic Quarantine Policy Works
Step 1 · Adversarial Binary
Unprofiled Execution
A copy of fio is spawned as /tmp/photo_sync in root cgroup doing 2,000 MB/s sequential writes. It has no cgroup pre-assignment.
Step 2 · Layer 1 Rules Miss
Rule Bypass
The pattern engine evaluates 20+ regex patterns (games, browsers, databases, sync tools). photo_sync matches zero rules and bypasses Layer 1.
Step 3 · Heuristic Quarantine
Layer 2 Isolation
The observer samples /proc/<pid>/io for 3 seconds. Seeing a write ratio >70% (2 GB/s), it quarantines photo_sync to tg/background!
Step 4 · Anti-Flapping Filter
False-Positive Prevention
A concurrent 15MB short burst is detected. Because active I/O is <5MB over the observation window, it is not quarantined (avoiding false penalties).
